Project status

The honest part

YulduzBola is a young project. What works, what is in testing and what is not there yet is all here.

✓Works today

  • 8 tasks, 16 badges, a reward shop, a family leaderboard
  • Parent panel: approval, bonuses, custom tasks and rewards
  • Three languages: Uzbek, Russian, English
  • No sign-up, no ads, no server: the data stays on the phone

◐In testing

  • The Android app is built (22 MB). All 10 screens pass an automated test, but it has not been widely tested on real phones yet.
  • Some labels are in Uzbek only for now

○Not yet

  • Not on Google Play
  • No iPhone version
  • No sync between phones and no notifications: the family shares one device
